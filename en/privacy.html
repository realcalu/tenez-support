Privacy policy

Tenez processes your tennis videos on your device. You do not need an account to use the app.

Who operates Tenez

Tenez is operated by honghui hong. For support, privacy questions or a data deletion request, email waraphongeon@gmail.com.

Videos, photos and microphone

With your permission, Tenez uses the camera to record tennis and the microphone to record court sound. You can decline microphone access and record without sound. Photos access lets you select videos for local analysis and save original recordings or finished highlights. You may grant access only to selected photos. Tenez does not upload your videos, photos or audio to us. Analysis and editing run on your device.

Optional Apple Health access

If you enable Apple Health, Tenez reads tennis workouts, including their dates, duration, source and identifiers, to display training time on your device. It does not write workouts. Video features work without Health access. Workout results are held in memory; legacy workout associations are kept in device-only storage excluded from backup. Tenez does not send Health data to us, use it for advertising, or store it in iCloud. Apple controls any syncing performed by the Health app itself.

Analytics, advertising and payments

This release has no advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics uploads or in-app feedback uploads. It does not use IDFA. App Store purchases and refunds are handled by Apple; Tenez does not receive your payment card details.

Local storage and your choices

Videos, highlights, editing drafts, dates and any court labels you enter remain in the app’s local library until you delete them. You can delete records and highlights in the app, or remove the app to remove its app data. Copies saved to Photos or shared to another app must be deleted in those destinations separately. iOS may back up ordinary app files according to your device settings; Health associations are excluded. You can change camera, microphone and Photos permissions in Settings, and revoke Health access in the Health app. Turning off the Health display clears the displayed workouts; it does not delete Apple Health records.

When you contact support

Email support is optional. We receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send through Gmail and use them to respond and troubleshoot. Please do not send private videos, Health records, passwords or payment details. Support email is normally deleted within 12 months after the issue is resolved, unless longer retention is required by law. You may request earlier deletion by email.

This website and external links

This public support website uses no advertising or analytics scripts and no contact form. Its hosting provider may process technical request data, such as IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure the website. Opening a link or sending email is subject to the selected browser, host or email provider’s own practices. No app library or Health data is attached when opening these pages.

Policy changes

We will update this page and its effective date if our practices change. A future feature that requires additional data will explain that use before it is enabled.
