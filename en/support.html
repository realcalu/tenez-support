Support

Need help with recording, importing, editing or saving a highlight?

Contact the developer

Email waraphongeon@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Tenez Rally version and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot with private information covered can help. Please do not send your Apple password, payment details or Health records.

Importing a video

Choose a tennis video already downloaded to Photos. If iCloud has only a cloud copy, first download it using Photos, then return to Tenez Rally. Check the app’s Photos permission if a selected video is unavailable.

Processing and editing

Keep Tenez Rally open while it processes a video. Suggested highlights may need adjustment; you can change their boundaries or add clips manually. Results depend on framing, lighting, audio and the original recording. Tenez Rally is a creative tool, not an official scoring or medical tool.

Saving and interrupted exports

Allow Photos access when saving. If an export says its save result is uncertain, check Photos before retrying to avoid duplicates. Keep the original video until you have checked your finished highlights. Free storage may be needed for rendering.

Apple Health

Health access is optional and only shows available tennis workouts. If no workouts appear, check Health permissions and whether tennis workouts exist in the Health app. Recording and editing remain available without it.

Purchases and refunds

For an App Store purchase or refund, use Apple’s Report a Problem. For app problems or privacy requests, contact the developer above.
